Privacy Policy

For NotePoket Android Application

Effective Date: November 11, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to NotePoket. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how the NotePoket Android application handles information when you use our app.

2. Information Collection

NotePoket is designed to let users create, edit, manage, and delete personal notes directly on their Android devices.

Notes and their related content are stored locally on your device. The app does not send your note content to a NotePoket server.

We do not require users to create an account or provide personal information to use the basic note-taking features.

3. Data Storage

Your notes are stored in the app's local storage on your device. We do not operate a cloud service for storing your notes.

Please be aware that uninstalling the app or clearing its application data may permanently delete locally stored notes. We recommend keeping your own backup if needed.

4. Data Sharing

NotePoket does not sell, rent, or share your personal notes with third parties.

If you choose to share a note using Android's sharing features, the information will be shared with the app or service you select.

5. Permissions

NotePoket only uses Android permissions necessary for the features you use. If a feature requires access to device storage, notifications, or other resources, Android will request the relevant permission where applicable.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the app and its locally stored information. However, no method of electronic storage is completely secure. Please protect your device with an appropriate screen lock and security settings.

7. Children's Privacy

NotePoket does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe personal information has been provided to us, please contact us.

8. Your Privacy Choices

You can edit or delete your notes directly within the application. You can also clear the application's data or uninstall the app to remove locally stored information.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
